Academic Knowledge Management - Where to Start?

Principles and tools to save hundreds of hours during research and writing

Proper management of research materials saves hundreds of hours during the writing phase. Here are the principles and tools I use:

1. Reference Management

I highly recommend using Zotero. It allows you to save articles with a single click, organize them into smart folders, and generate automatic bibliographies.

  • Top Tip: You can change the entire citation style of a full document with one click.
  • Tutorial: Watch my full Zotero tutorial (Hebrew)

2. Note-Taking and Summaries

Don't rely on memory. Write summaries for articles in a structure that includes:

  • Article title and full APA citation.
  • Core themes and literature review abstract.
  • Findings, methodology, and discussion.
  • Personal notes and reflections.

3. Backup, Storage, and Version Control

  • Cloud Sync: Ensure your folder is synced to Google Drive or OneDrive. Get your fingers used to hitting Ctrl+S.
  • File Naming: Save versions by date in a sortable format (e.g., 2024-05-12_Chapter1).

Thoughts on Efficient Research

  • Curiosity as a Driver: Follow topics that spark interest, but always return to your core task.
  • Source Mining: Utilize bibliographies of the articles you read to find new sources.
  • Embracing Technology (AI): AI tools can significantly streamline your workflow. Always use them with a critical eye and verify outputs.
