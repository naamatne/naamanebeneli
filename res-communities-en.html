Why Online Communities?

Core characteristics, benefits, and challenges.

Defining the Online Community

  • Virtual Interaction: Communication via digital platforms.
  • Common Ground: Shared interest, goal, or identity.
  • Freedom of Choice: Autonomy to decide engagement level.

Key Benefits

  • Strengthening ties regardless of geography.
  • Providing empathy and peer-to-peer support.
  • Collective problem-solving and mutual aid.
