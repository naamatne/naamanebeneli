Research Interests

Focusing on micro-macro integration and the evolution of community intervention.

Technology & AI

Exploring the integration of AI tools and digital solutions in social services.

Gerontechnology

Technology implementation in care services for the elderly and family caregivers.

Crisis Communities

Online support networks during times of national crisis and crisis intervention.

Social Network Analysis

Using SNA methods to understand support systems and community resilience.

About Me

I am a professional social worker and a PhD candidate at Bar-Ilan University, where I am also a recipient of the President's Scholarship for Excellence. My work bridges the gap between grassroots community methods and macro-level social policy. I hold an MSW (Summa Cum Laude) from Ariel University and serve as an Editorial Fellow at the "Society & Welfare" (Hevra uRevacha) journal.
