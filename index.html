About Me

I am a PhD Candidate and researcher in Social Work. My research focuses on the "Community Approach" within social services, exploring the bridging of micro and macro practices. With extensive professional experience as a Community Social Worker and program manager for senior citizens, I aim to integrate field-based insights with academic rigor to enhance social welfare systems.

Alumna of the Summer Institute in Computational Social Science (SICSS-Sharjah/ESN, 2026).
