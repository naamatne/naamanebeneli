About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Social Work at Bar-Ilan University and an academic researcher. My doctoral dissertation, "Breaking Down the Wall? Bridging Micro and Macro in Social Work," focuses on the perceptions and practices of the community approach in the Caregivers of Elderly Citizens Program. I hold an MSW (Summa Cum Laude) from Ariel University and a BSW from Ben-Gurion and Ariel Universities. I have extensive professional experience as a Community Social Worker, managing programs for senior citizens, and leading community initiatives.
