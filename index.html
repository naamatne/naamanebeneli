About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Social Work and an academic researcher. My research focuses on the "Community Approach" within social services, exploring the bridging of micro and macro practices. With extensive professional experience as a Community Social Worker and program manager for senior citizens, I aim to integrate field-based insights with academic rigor to enhance social welfare systems.
